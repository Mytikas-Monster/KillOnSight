Kill on Sight (KoS) - changelog
================================

1.4.6  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

A flat ask, and the money rule spelled out in the game.

  * Changed: every bounty asks 3g. The old +5g for each further guild member
    killed is gone; existing unfunded rows are moved to 3g once on login.
  * Added: a first-run notice, shown once per account on install/login. It
    spells out the rule in game - a name or pending job is not a bounty, the
    gold must be in the guild bank, and nobody should hunt until the row says
    "bounty" - and cannot be closed until "I have read and understand this"
    is ticked and the acknowledgement button is pressed.
  * Changed: the window's top line now says there is no bounty without a
    guild-bank deposit and not to hunt until it is funded.
  * Changed: the Add box's blank gold field now falls back to 3g, not 5g.

1.4.5  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

Map pins now clean themselves up.

  * Changed: a pin retires 30 minutes after the last sighting/death, on both
    the world map and the minimap, so old kills do not clutter people's maps.
  * Changed: a later sighting (including one at the bounty click) starts the
    30 minutes again.
  * Unchanged: the kill and its "last known location" stay on the board and in
    /kos where; only the map pins are removed.
  * /kos pins now states the 30 minute limit.

1.4.4  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

The last known location is now a pin on your maps.

  * Added: one pin per spotted killer on the world map and the minimap, drawn
    from the stored coordinates by HereBeDragons-Pins. These are addon-only
    pins: WoW has no shared world marker an addon can place for other players.
  * Added: /kos pins forces a refresh and reports how many pins are on each
    map.
  * Safety: a "nearby" point is drawn in rust and its tooltip says it is not
    an exact killer pin. A row with no readable spot gets no pin at all.
  * Safety: world map pins are removed in Forever's gamepad UI (the map canvas
    is protected there); minimap pins stay. Input-mode changes are watched.
  * Bundled HereBeDragons-Pins-2.0, with Bootstrap.lua loading Blizzard_WorldMap
    before the library hooks the map canvas.
  * No shared world marker API and no SetTexCoord; pins use one flat texture.

1.4.3  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

Position capture now uses the same mapping library Olympus uses.

  * Added: bundled LibStub, CallbackHandler-1.0 and HereBeDragons-2.0 under
    libs\, loaded from the .toc before KillOnSight.lua.
  * Changed: when C_Map cannot read a visible target, mouseover or nameplate
    directly, HereBeDragons converts that unit's world position into the
    observer's current Zone or Micro map. This is the Olympus-style path.
  * Safety: the conversion is only accepted for an open Zone/Micro map, with
    out-of-bounds points refused. A different map or bad data stays unseen and
    still falls back to "last seen near where I died" at the death itself.
  * No raw UnitPosition / GetMapPosFromWorldPos guesswork was introduced.

1.4.2  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

The location is now part of the window and the guild announcement.

  * Added: "LAST KNOWN LOCATION:" now sits at the foot of the enemy list, under
    the ENEMY / BOUNTY / KILLS / STATE rows. It follows the selected enemy and
    never disappears just because there is no bounty yet.
  * Changed: /kos announce now says "last known location:" explicitly in the
    guild message, instead of only trailing the position after the bounty line.
  * Changed: the selected enemy's detail panel also labels that line "last
    known location:".

1.4.1  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

The location now actually shows up in chat.

  * Fixed: the normal "on the board, no bounty funded" notice was the one line
    that dropped the last known spot. It now ends with "last known location:
    ..." like the pending, funded and guild-announce lines.
  * Fixed: the death line now explicitly says "Where you were killed: ...", so
    the observer's fallback is labelled honestly as "last seen near where I
    died" rather than as a made-up exact pin.
  * Added: /kos where [name] prints the stored spot and the raw map reads from
    this client. If no spot was readable, it says so and shows which reads
    failed (player / target / mouseover).
  * Fixed: removed the UnitPosition-to-map fallback. On this client it could
    convert an unreadable target into an invented point, so the addon fails
    closed and falls back only to the observer's own death location.

1.4.0  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

Where he was, a fairer split, and a badge over his head.

  * Added: the last known position is captured when a guild member dies. WoW
    cannot report an arbitrary player's location, so the addon uses the killer's
    own position only while he is visible as a target, mouseover or nameplate.
    If he is already gone, it records where the observer died and says
    "last seen near where I died" - it never invents a pin.
  * Added: that spot travels with the bounty on the guild row, appears in the
    death and bounty chat lines, the pending-job line and the detail panel. A
    later visible sighting (including one at the job click) replaces an older
    one; "nearby" is never reported as exact, on this client or a guildmate's.
  * Added: a small KoS badge on the nameplate of anyone with money on his head.
    It is a nameplate icon, not a world marker: only players running the addon
    see it. Unfunded, paid and refunded targets are not marked.
  * Changed: the split is now 70/30 - the killer gets 70%, the guild bank keeps
    30%. Existing profiles still on the old 10% move to 30% once; a custom cut
    is left alone.
  * Changed: the minimum a death asks for is 3g instead of 5g. Existing profiles
    still on the old 5g default move to 3g once; a custom value is left alone.
    Further kills still add 5g each.

1.3.0  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

Right-click starts the bounty job itself.

  * Added: right-click an enemy's name in either list (the death feed or the
    board) to start a bounty job instantly. /kos bounty <name> does the same
    from the keyboard. Left-click still only selects him.
  * Added: the job is stored on the entry and shared with the guild at once, so
    it survives a /reload and every member sees "pending job" before any money
    moves. The window counts pending jobs in its subtitle; the rows and the
    feed say "pending job". A shared job never arms another client's bank
    watcher and never invents money.
  * Changed: arming with the Fund button also creates a pending job, so every
    way of starting a bounty says the same thing. Only a verified rise in the
    guild bank's own gold turns the job live and pays anything out.
  * Changed: after a bounty is paid or refunded, the killer's next kill waits
    unfunded until somebody starts a new job - the old job does not carry over.
  * Fixed: reopening a settled bounty now clears the old refund details as well
    as the old payout.
  * Fixed: the two name rows now register for right-clicks. Without that, the
    client only sends left-clicks to the handler and right-click did nothing in
    game even though it worked in the test harness.

1.2.0  (19 Nov 2026)
--------------------

The board now remembers who killed whom, and a killer's name is a way into the
bounty flow.

  * Added: a guild death feed under the board - killer, victim, when, and the
    bounty state. It is shared over the same guild channel as the board, so
    every member running the addon sees the same history, including deaths that
    happened before somebody installed it (the newest 50 travel in the digest).
  * Added: click a killer's name in the feed to select him on the board and set
    a bounty. If the board has forgotten him, the click puts him back with the
    victim from the death row; if his old bounty was paid or refunded, the click
    starts a fresh one. Nothing is paid by the click: Fund still asks for the
    deposit first, and the bounty only goes live when the guild bank's own gold
    shows it.
  * Added: Mine / All on the feed, so you can show just your own deaths or the
    whole guild's. /kos kills lists the same history in chat; /kos kills mine
    limits it to deaths of yours.
  * Added: a death row is history only. It never creates or resurrects a board
    entry by itself, so a killer another member deliberately deleted stays
    deleted until somebody clicks his name.
  * Changed: /kos reset wipes the board and the bounties but keeps the guild
    death history. The window is taller (540x760) to fit the feed between the
    board and the detail panel, still on the game's own frame with Forever's
    inset art.

1.1.1  (18 Nov 2026)
--------------------

A death now registers even when the client leaves part of the recap out.

  * Fixed: deaths were being thrown away by over-strict reading. The client
    does not always fill every field of its death recap, and a detail it left
    out was treated as proof that no player killed you. Now:
      - a float timestamp counts (it is floored, not rejected);
      - a killing blow with no event name still counts;
      - a sourceFlags the client did not give is not a refusal;
      - a destGUID this addon cannot parse no longer vetoes your own death
        (only a different, parseable victim does);
      - a GUID id longer than 16 hex digits is still the same player;
      - no sourceGUID: the killer is looked up by the name the row gives.
  * Fixed: an unreadable IsInInstance answer no longer counts as "you are in an
    instance" - only a clear yes does.
  * Added: a 2 s death watch, so a build that raises no PLAYER_DEAD at all still
    records who killed you. It uses the same dedupe (killer@stamp), so the event
    and the watch never count the same death twice.
  * Added: retries at 0.3/1/2/4/8 s (was 0.3/1/2), for a recap that lands late.
  * Added: one chat line at each death - "X killed you - added to the kill on
    sight list (5g asked)", or, when a player killed you and nothing was
    recorded, "died to X, but nothing was recorded: <why>". Once per death.
  * Added: /kos recap - a diagnostic that prints C_DeathRecap, what
    HasRecapEvents() and GetRecapEvents() answered, the first three rows with
    every field, your own GUID and whether it parses, dead/instance state, and
    what the addon made of the last death. Paste it when a death does not count.

1.1.0  (18 Nov 2026)
--------------------

FIRST RELEASE.

What it does
  * An enemy player who kills a guild member is put on a guild-wide board
    automatically: name, class, how many guild members he has killed, and who
    he killed. Instances (battlegrounds and arenas) are ignored by default.
  * The board is shared live with every guildmate who runs the addon, over the
    guild addon channel. Rows merge by GUID and Lamport stamp, so two members
    seeing the same kill end up with one row, not two.
  * A window on the game's own portrait frame - the Character/Professions frame
    - with the game's inset panels, the game's buttons and Forever's own art,
    the same pieces the Happy Camper addon draws with. /kos opens it.

The rule the addon enforces
  * A bounty exists only when its money is already in the guild bank. A death
    creates an UNFUNDED row ("no bounty is funded"); it pays nothing until
    somebody puts the gold in.
  * The deposit is verified against the guild bank's own balance
    (GetGuildBankMoney), never against a promise. Part deposits add up: 3g and
    then 2g funds a 5g bounty. If the balance cannot be read, no bounty is
    issued.
  * The killer is owed 90%; the guild keeps 10%. The cut is configurable
    (/kos cut 0-50).
  * Default bounty is 5g (/kos reward <gold>), and each further kill by the
    same enemy adds another 5g to what a bounty would need.

Money that has to move by hand
  * No addon can move gold here: the deposit and the payout are done by a
    person at the bank. The addon verifies, records and remembers.
  * Anyone in the guild may fund an entry, record a payout, or remove an entry.
    An entry with money in it cannot be removed until that money is settled.
  * An unclaimed bounty goes back to whoever paid it in after one week (never
    to a killer). The refund is two-step: the addon marks it due, and a person
    confirms it after handing the gold over.

Saying it out loud
  * The guild bank cannot refund by itself, so an unhanded payment is stated in
    officer chat and then keeps being stated until it is actually paid.
  * Chat needs a real key press or click on this client, so the officer-chat
    line is always a click (Officers button, /kos officers) - never a timer.
  * Officer chat is used when this character is the guild master or first rank
    under him; otherwise the line goes to guild chat. /kos officermode
    auto|officer|guild overrides that.

Commands
  /kos                        open the window
  /kos add <name> [gold] [why] put a name on the board by hand
  /kos fund <name>            arm a bounty, then deposit that much in the bank
  /kos paid <name>            record that the killer was handed his share
  /kos refund <name>          mark an unclaimed bounty as going back
  /kos handed <name>          confirm the refund was actually handed over
  /kos owed                   what has not been handed over yet
  /kos officers               say that in officer/guild chat (one click)
  /kos remove <name> [force]  take an entry off the board
  /kos reward <gold>          what a new bounty asks for (default 5)
  /kos cut <percent>          0-50, the bank's share (default 10)
  /kos days <days>            how long an unclaimed bounty waits (default 7)
  /kos list | status | sync | announce | share | notices | minimap | reset

Notes
  * The combat-log event is restricted on this client and is never registered.
    Deaths are read from the client's own death recap (PLAYER_DEAD +
    C_DeathRecap), and your own killing blows from PARTY_KILL.
  * Names are "First Surname" on this server; "Name-Realm" is refused.
  * The guild bank balance has to have been read at least once (open the bank
    once) before a deposit can be verified.
